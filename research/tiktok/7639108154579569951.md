## Observations for Agentarium

- The visible metaphor is **HQ / departments / labs**, not a pure Sims-like world.
- “Forge Labs” acts like an operational department or room.
- New work appears as **intake forms**.
- A glowing building/department indicates pending work.
- Clicking a department opens a more specific workspace.
- Department contains named sub-agents: `Blueprint`, `Relay`, `Pulse`, `Echo`, `Ember`.
- There is a “Blueprint Center” for reviewing intake forms.
- This suggests the system is closer to an **interactive operational dashboard with spatial/game metaphors** than a free-roaming AI society.

## Product implications

For Agentarium, prioritize:

1. HQ/map as spatial navigation.
2. Departments/rooms as operational lanes.
3. Glowing/animated rooms when work is pending.
4. Intake forms/requests as first-class objects.
5. Department-specific sub-agent teams.
6. Drill-down panels/centers from each room.
7. Real business/privacy-safe data boundaries.
