## New product primitives from this tour

The tour adds several primitives Agentarium should model:

1. **Lab type** — factory, research, communications, archive, armory, treasury, media, war room, music, content, etc.
2. **Lab backend** — Etsy, Printify, Printful, Suppleful, Fiverr, TikTok, YouTube, OpenClaw, Obsidian-like memory, etc.
3. **Lab metrics** — revenue, ad spend, cost/month, orders, output count, pending work, ROI.
4. **Agent role** — steward, researcher, factory operator, designer, communicator, DJ, writer, reviewer.
5. **Feedback memory** — approval/reject/iterate notes become durable behavioral memory.
6. **Daily/weekly strategy loop** — agents review performance and propose improvements.
7. **Cost governance** — dedicated treasury surface for API/model/tool spend.
8. **Unified communications** — comments/email/messages feed into one deck.
9. **Publishing stations** — drag/drop or queue assets for scheduled publishing.
10. **Fulfillment stations** — print-on-demand or product backend handles shipping/delivery.

## Product implications for Agentarium

This tour pushes Agentarium beyond “visual task board” into an **AI business operating system** with connected spatial labs.

MVP should still stay small, but the architecture should reserve room for:

- Treasury / cost control
- Archives / durable memory
- Feedback / training console
- War Room / strategy review
- Communications / unified inbox
- Media Bay / scheduling
- Factory labs with real platform backends

## Tone / ethics note

Do not copy the source’s hostile language about replacing people or enslaving agents. Agentarium should frame this as:

- making AI operations visible
- improving human supervision
- automating repeatable workflows responsibly
- tracking cost/risk/quality
- protecting customers and platforms with audit/approval boundaries
